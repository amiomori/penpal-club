Privacy Policy (Draft / 公開前要確認)

Last draft: October 2026. This is a preliminary explanation, not a finalized legal policy. Operator details and retention periods must be finalized before public launch.

Data collected

We collect account email, nickname, country, languages, interests, private shipping address, letter exchange records, support messages and, only with consent, uploaded photographs and saved translations.

Purposes and access

We use this information for account management, letter forwarding, support, fraud prevention and consented photo/translation features. Shipping addresses are not shared with matched members. The operator and necessary delivery/service providers may access information required to fulfill services.

Photographs

Community photographs remain private until reviewed and approved by the operator. Do not upload visible addresses, full names, private letter contents or other people's information without permission. Approved images become publicly visible and may be copied by visitors.

AI translation

When enabled, images are processed by an external AI provider for handwriting recognition and translation. Only choose to save if you wish to retain the letter. Do not upload confidential material without permission. Specific provider, retention, international transfer and deletion terms require final review.

Your choices

Contact the operator to request access, correction, deletion or withdrawal of gallery consent. The operator's contact email and response procedure must be confirmed before launch.

Contact

Contact form (members only until public contact delivery is configured).
