Eligibility

Penpal Club is intended for adults aged 18 and older. Members must supply accurate account and shipping information.

Physical letters

Mystery Letter is a physical handwritten letter exchange. Letters are sent to a confirmed hub, paired only after receipt, and forwarded only after both required payments are verified. Do not send items before receiving verified hub instructions.

No dangerous, illegal or prohibited items. Respect others' privacy; do not publish letter contents, identifying information or photographs without appropriate permission. Letters may be delayed, rejected or investigated for safety or postal compliance.
