Take the long way to someone.

  1. If you'd like to join, write a kind letter to an unknown guest.
  2. Don't include your name, contact details, or private information.
  3. Fold your letter and place it in the collection box.
  4. We'll shuffle the letters and hand one to each participating guest today.

One letter given, one letter received.
Thank you for sharing a little kindness.
